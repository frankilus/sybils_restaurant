Our story

She sold bread to make rent. She built an institution.

Sybil Bernard-Kerrutt immigrated from Guyana and worked as a hairdresser in New York. To make rent and support her family, she started selling bread to friends. The bread became a business. The business became Sybil’s.

In 1976 she opened her first bakery in Jamaica, Queens. The flagship followed on Liberty Ave — that striking yellow-and-turquoise triangular building at 133rd St that you can spot from blocks away, in the heart of Little Guyana. Sybil passed in 2000; her family runs it still, on the original recipes.
